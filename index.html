About the Salon

A cheerful, family‑run salon tucked inside the Hilton Westchase.

For years, Dan Phyl's has been Houston's quiet little favorite — the kind of place where the chairs are warm, the conversation is warmer, and you leave looking (and feeling) like the best version of you. Whether you're popping in for a quick trim or settling in for a full color, you'll be treated like family from the moment you walk in.
